Welcome

Falls are one of the leading causes of injury and loss of independence among older adults. Traditional solutions (wearable buttons, pull cords) often fail because a person may be unable to press them after falling. Modern computer vision systems can watch for falls automatically and alert caregivers within seconds — without requiring the senior to wear anything.

This tutorial teaches you the current state of the art. You will learn:

  • Why falls are such a serious problem
  • How computer vision systems detect falls in real time
  • Key algorithms (pose estimation, classification, privacy-preserving pipelines)
  • Real systems already deployed or researched (including University of Alberta work)
  • Major challenges: privacy, false alarms, lighting, and edge computing
  • Where the field is heading
How to use this tutorial: Work through the pages in order using the top navigation. Each page has a short audio narration. The whole tutorial takes about 20–25 minutes.

Learning Objectives

After completing this tutorial you will be able to:

  1. Explain the clinical and economic impact of falls in older adults.
  2. Describe the main pipeline of a vision-based fall detection system.
  3. Compare pose-estimation approaches (MediaPipe, YOLO-based, skeleton + LSTM).
  4. Discuss privacy-preserving techniques used in real systems.
  5. Identify current limitations and promising research directions.
